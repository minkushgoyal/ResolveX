# ResolveX Roles

## Platform Roles

- Resident: can use community, complaints, visitors, services, notices, amenities, profile, and household features.
- Flat Owner: resident with owner-level household permissions.
- Tenant: resident with society-configurable access to bills and household settings.
- Society Admin: manages society-wide data, residents, complaints, bills, notices, and settings.
- Society Manager: operates daily workflows and staff assignment.
- Security Guard: handles visitor, delivery, and gate workflows.
- Maintenance Staff: updates assigned complaints and uploads proof.
- Service Provider: receives and updates service bookings.
- Super Admin: global platform access.

## Household Permissions

Owners and co-owners can invite members, remove members, manage join requests, and view household bills. Family members can raise complaints, use services, participate in the community, receive notices, and book amenities. Tenant permissions are controlled by society settings.
